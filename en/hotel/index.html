FAQ

Common questions

Are you licensed?

Yes. Our Tsim Sha Tsui campus holds an Animal Boarding Establishment Licence issued by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.

Is someone with the dogs around the clock?

Staff are on duty daily from 8:30am to 8:30pm, and every room has its own 24-hour CCTV. We send regular updates with photos and videos during the stay.

What should I bring?

Vaccination record and dog licence, food for the whole stay, any prescription food or medicine with feeding times, and one or two toys or a familiar-smelling item.

Can family visit during the stay?

Yes. Visiting hours are 16:00 to 18:00 daily; please book at least one day ahead. Up to 2 visitors for 30 minutes.

How are rates calculated?

Rates depend on your dog's size and the length of stay, with overnight and day-care options. Message us on WhatsApp for the latest rate card.
