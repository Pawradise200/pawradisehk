FAQ

Common questions before a stay

Are you licensed?

Yes. Our Tsim Sha Tsui campus holds an Animal Boarding Establishment Licence issued by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.

Is someone looking after the dogs around the clock?

Staff are on duty daily from 8:30am to 8:30pm, looking after the dogs' routine and activities, and every room has its own 24-hour CCTV. We also send regular updates with photos and videos during the stay.

Can family or friends visit during the stay?

Yes. Visiting hours are 16:00 to 18:00 daily. Visits must be booked by the parent at least one day ahead, for up to 2 visitors and 30 minutes.

How do you handle meals?

Please bring food for the whole stay; we can handle dry, wet, fresh and raw diets. Breakfast is at 09:30 and dinner at 18:30; if you pack lunch, we serve it during activity time. Please note feeding times for any prescription food or medicine.

What should I prepare before check-in?

A valid vaccination record and dog licence, food for the stay, any prescription food or medicine, and one or two toys or familiar-smelling items. Please bring belly bands or diapers if needed.

How does your care work?

Every dog has a risk assessment before the stay and is grouped by risk rating and personality. Dogs join supervised social time each day, rest in their own room, and follow a set daily schedule.

How do you handle toileting?

Toilet times are 08:30 and 20:00 daily, and rooms have pee pads. Please bring belly bands or diapers if your dog needs them.

Are big and small dogs separated in play areas?

We arrange suitable activities and playmates based on each dog's risk rating, personality and how they are doing on the day.
