FAQ

Common questions

How do I book a trial day?

Message us on WhatsApp for the next open day and available places. Your place is confirmed once booked.

Do parents stay the whole time?

Parents join the mini-class. During the assessment, parents step out briefly so teachers can see how the dog behaves with other dogs.

My dog barks at other dogs or is shy. Can we join?

Yes. Teachers adjust distance and pace to your dog's state and never force interaction.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Please let us know on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before the trial.
